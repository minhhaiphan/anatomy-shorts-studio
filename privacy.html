Anatomy Shorts Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Anatomy Shorts Studio ("the Studio") is a personal tool operated by Minh Hai. It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only by its operator. This policy explains what the Studio does with data from TikTok.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when you connect your account.
  • Basic account information TikTok returns for the connected account (your open ID, display name and avatar), and the creator information needed to post (for example the privacy levels you can choose and the longest video you can post).
  • The status of each upload (its publish ID and whether it succeeded).

2. How we use it

Only to upload the videos you choose to your own TikTok account (an upload starts only when you press the upload button and confirm, or run the Studio's upload command), to show you which account is connected and which posting options you have, and to show you whether an upload succeeded. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Where it is stored

On the operator's computer only. The tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in the Studio's local configuration; an upload log (video, time, publish ID) is kept in the Studio's local project files. TikTok data is not sent to any server of ours, because there is none.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with anyone. Your videos and their captions are sent only to TikTok, when you upload them. The Studio uses AI services (Anthropic, OpenAI, ElevenLabs) to write and narrate videos from the article text you give it; no TikTok data is sent to these services.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until you disconnect TikTok in the Studio's settings, which deletes them. You can also remove the Studio's access at any time in your TikTok app settings. Upload logs stay with the project they belong to and are deleted with it.

6. Children

The Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
