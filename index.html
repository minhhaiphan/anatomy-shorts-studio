Anatomy Shorts Studio

A personal video studio for short explainer videos.

Anatomy Shorts Studio turns an article into a short explainer video (vertical 9:16): a script, a narrated voice, captions, illustrations and diagrams, rendered on the creator's own computer.

When the creator starts an upload — the Send to TikTok button, which asks for confirmation, or the Studio's upload command — the finished video is uploaded to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API: as a draft in the TikTok inbox (the creator finishes and publishes it in the TikTok app), or as a post with the privacy level the creator chooses. Nothing is uploaded unless the creator starts it. Direct posts are labelled as AI-generated content; for drafts, the Studio reminds the creator to turn on TikTok's AI-generated label when finishing the post.

The studio is a single-user tool run by its operator; there is no public sign-up.
